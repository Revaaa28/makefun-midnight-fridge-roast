# Candidates — Midnight Fridge Roast

Ten candidates, ranked by hand against the judge brief in the brief's own order:

1. One-line roast of a smart fridge that refuses to open after midnight
2. A real joke with a surprising turn/punchline, not a mild complaint
3. Tight — one line, reads cleanly aloud, no wasted words, no second joke
4. The midnight refusal itself is the target — the laugh comes from the fridge's petty rule, not generic smart-home grumbling
5. Sounds like something a person would actually say out loud at the fridge door

## Ranked candidates

1. **After midnight the fridge only opens for 'authorized users.' I live alone. The fridge has never said who they are.**
2. Midnight hits and my smart fridge turns into my mother: 'The kitchen is closed. We have food at home.' I AM home.
3. My fridge has a curfew. I am 34. The fridge is 2. Somehow the toddler appliance is parenting me.
4. My fridge locks at midnight like my leftovers are in witness protection and I'm the threat.
5. It locks at midnight to 'protect my health goals.' Sir, you are a box of cold air with a light inside.
6. The fridge refuses to open after midnight but happily lights up to watch me beg. That's not a feature, that's a hobby.
7. Midnight: the fridge locks, displays my step count, and judges. I just wanted the pie, not a performance review.
8. It's midnight, so the fridge has locked itself and started a union meeting about my snack habits.
9. After midnight my fridge becomes a nightclub bouncer: 'You're not on the list.' I BOUGHT the list.
10. The fridge won't open after midnight. It says the cheese needs its beauty sleep. The cheese is 40.

## Ranking rationale

- **#1 wins** — the refusal rule itself is the joke (criterion 4), the 'authorized users' angle is specific to a smart fridge, and the last sentence is a genuine twist (criterion 2) in one tight breath (criterion 3).
- **#2 is runner-up** on relatability but leans on a stock mother line.
- **#3 is tight** but the curfew framing is less smart-fridge-specific.
- The rest either stack a second joke (#7, #10), aim at smart-home grumbling generally (#6, #8), or lose the midnight rule (#4's lock is generic).

Winning line length: 114 characters.
